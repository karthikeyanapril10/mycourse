⚠️ THE BRUTAL TRUTH: Yes, They Still Ask DSA at 12+ Years

What No One Tells You:

Question: "I have 12 years experience building production systems. Do I really need to grind LeetCode?"
Answer: YES. Unfortunately, yes.

The Reality Check:

Company Type DSA in Interview? Weight Difficulty
FAANG (Google, Meta, Amazon, etc.) ✅ YES 40-50% Medium/Hard
Top Startups (Razorpay, Swiggy, etc.) ✅ YES 30-40% Medium
Product Companies (Adobe, Atlassian, etc.) ✅ YES 30-40% Medium
Service Companies (TCS, Infosys, etc.) ⚠️ Maybe 10-20% Easy/Medium
Smaller Startups ⚠️ 50/50 20% Easy/Medium

Typical Senior Interview (12+ years):

Round 1: DSA Coding (2 problems, 45 min) ← YES, still happens
Round 2: System Design (60 min)
Round 3: DSA + Architecture (1 hard + design)
Round 4: Leadership + Behavioral
Round 5: Hiring Manager (vision + depth)

What Changes vs Junior Developers:

Aspect Junior (0-3 years) Senior (12+ years)
DSA Weight 80% DSA, 20% System Design 40% DSA, 60% System Design
Difficulty Easy/Medium Medium/Hard (same!)
Time to Solve Can struggle, get hints Expected to solve fast
Code Quality Brute force OK Must optimize, clean code
Expectations "Can they code?" "Can they code AND architect?"

The Frustrating Part:
- ❌ Your 12 years building payment systems? Doesn't matter for DSA round.
- ❌ Your experience debugging 100M+ row databases? Doesn't help with "reverse a binary tree."
- ❌ Your on-call production firefighting skills? Won't save you from "find K closest points."

Why Do They Still Do This?
1. Filter at scale - Easy to compare candidates with a standard test
2. Proxy for problem-solving - Can you think algorithmically under pressure?
3. Industry standard - Everyone does it, so they do it too
4. Lazy interviewing - Easier than custom practical tests

What You MUST Do (No Choice):

✅ 1. Accept the reality - Fighting it won't help. Just prepare.
✅ 2. Grind LeetCode - 100-150 problems (Medium focus, some Hard)
✅ 3. Focus on patterns - Two pointers, sliding window, DFS/BFS, DP
✅ 4. Practice on whiteboard - Not just on IDE with autocomplete
✅ 5. Explain while coding - They want to hear your thought process

Time Investment (Be Realistic):
- If you coded algorithms recently: 2-3 weeks prep (1-2 hours/day)
- If you haven't touched DSA in years: 6-8 weeks prep (2 hours/day)
- If you never learned it properly: 3-4 months (3 hours/day)

Your Advantage as a Senior:
- ✅ Better at explaining trade-offs (time vs space complexity)
- ✅ Faster at pattern recognition (seen more code patterns)
- ✅ Better at asking clarifying questions (product thinking)
- ✅ Cleaner code (variable names, edge cases, error handling)

What You Should Practice:

Top 20 Patterns (Must Know):
1. Two Pointers (merge sorted arrays, remove duplicates)
2. Sliding Window (longest substring, max sum subarray)
3. Binary Search (search rotated array, find peak)
4. DFS/BFS (tree traversal, graph connectivity)
5. Dynamic Programming (knapsack, longest subsequence)
6. HashMap/HashSet (two sum, group anagrams)
7. Stack/Queue (valid parentheses, min stack)
8. Linked List (reverse, detect cycle)
9. Trees (LCA, serialize/deserialize)
10. Graphs (shortest path, topological sort)
11. Heaps (K closest points, merge K sorted)
12. Trie (prefix search, autocomplete)
13. Backtracking (permutations, N-queens)
14. Greedy (interval scheduling, gas station)
15. Bit Manipulation (single number, counting bits)
16. Union Find (connected components, redundant connection)
17. Sorting (merge sort, quick select)
18. String Manipulation (palindrome, anagram)
19. Matrix (spiral traverse, rotate image)
20. Intervals (merge intervals, meeting rooms)

Sample Questions You'll Face (12+ Years Level):

Easy-Medium (Warm-up round):
- "Given two sorted arrays, merge them in-place."
- "Find the K most frequent elements in an array."
- "Implement LRU Cache."

Medium (Main rounds):
- "Design a URL shortener (data structure + algorithm)."
- "Find longest substring without repeating characters."
- "Serialize and deserialize a binary tree."
- "Word ladder transformation (shortest path)."

Hard (If going for Staff+ or FAANG):
- "Median of two sorted arrays (O(log(min(m,n))))."
- "Trapping rain water (histogram variant)."
- "Edit distance (Levenshtein, full DP)."
