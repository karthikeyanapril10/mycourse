💡 Pro Tips

Resume Customization

  • Use keywords from job description - ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) scan for these
  • Quantify everything - "Reduced MTTR by 40%" is better than "Improved response time"
  • Action verbs - Led, Designed, Implemented, Optimized, Reduced, Achieved
  • Tailor for each role - Emphasize different projects based on what they need

Cover Letter Success

  • First paragraph - Why you're writing, the role, brief credibility
  • Middle paragraphs - Match your experience to their needs (use their language)
  • Final paragraph - Enthusiasm + call to action
  • Keep it to 1 page - Hiring managers spend 30 seconds max
  • Proofread 3 times - Typos kill applications

Interview Strategy

  • The first answer sets the tone - Start strong with confidence
  • STAR format for behavioral - Situation, Task, Action, Result (always include Result!)
  • Ask clarifying questions - Shows you think before coding/answering
  • Think out loud - In technical exercises, explain your thought process
  • It's okay to say "I don't know" - Follow with "but here's how I'd find out"
  • Ask questions - Interviews are two-way; you're evaluating them too

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Badmouthing current/former employers
  • ❌ Memorizing answers word-for-word (sounds robotic)
  • ❌ Not preparing questions to ask them
  • ❌ Failing to research the company
  • ❌ Not following up after interview
  • ❌ Accepting first offer without negotiating
  • ❌ Lying or exaggerating (always gets caught)

📞 Next Steps After Getting Interest

  1. Recruiter Call (15-30 min)
    - Verify role details, compensation range, process
    - Ask timeline and next steps
    - Confirm your interest level

  2. Technical Screen (45-60 min)
    - Usually coding + system design
    - Review Technical Q&A guide beforehand
    - Have your development environment ready

  3. On-site/Virtual On-site (4-6 hours)
    - Multiple rounds: coding, system design, behavioral, culture fit
    - Review all materials the night before
    - Bring questions for each interviewer

  4. Offer Stage
    - Review total compensation (base, bonus, equity, benefits)
    - Don't accept immediately - take 24-48 hours
    - Negotiate if below market rate
    - Get everything in writing
