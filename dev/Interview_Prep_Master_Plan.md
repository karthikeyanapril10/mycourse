## 🎓 **Learning Tips**

### **For Code:**
- **Don't just read code** — write it from scratch
- **Don't copy-paste** — type it out yourself
- **Test your code** — run it, see errors, fix them
- **Explain every line** — what does it do, why is it needed?

### **For Architecture:**
- **Draw diagrams** — boxes, arrows, labels
- **Explain flow** — request comes in, goes here, then there
- **Identify bottlenecks** — where can it fail, slow down?
- **Consider trade-offs** — why this approach over others?

### **For Interviews:**
- **Think out loud** — interviewers want to see your process
- **Ask clarifying questions** — requirements, constraints, scale
- **Start simple** — basic solution first, then optimize
- **Handle edge cases** — empty input, max values, null, errors
- **Measure impact** — always quantify (40%, 500ms → 50ms, etc.)

### **For Confidence:**
- **Practice daily** — even 30 minutes helps
- **Record yourself** — watch/listen back, improve
- **Mock with friends** — peer interviews are gold
- **Celebrate small wins** — finished a section? Great!
- **Don't compare** — your journey is unique

## 🚨 **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

1. **Cramming the night before** → Study consistently, not last minute
2. **Memorizing without understanding** → Explain "why", not just "what"
3. **Skipping practice** → Reading ≠ doing, code it yourself
4. **Not timing yourself** → Real interviews have time limits
5. **Giving up too early** → "I don't know" is better than silence, reason through it
6. **Not asking questions** → Interviewers expect you to clarify
7. **Over-explaining** → Keep answers concise (3-5 min max)
8. **Ignoring weak areas** → Review what you don't know, not what you do
9. **Not preparing questions** → Always have 3-5 questions ready
10. **Poor storytelling** → Use STAR format for behavioral questions
