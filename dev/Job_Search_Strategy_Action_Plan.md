# Job Search Strategy: 8-Week Action Plan

> **Goal:** ₹35-40 LPA Staff Engineer role in 8 weeks (or less)

---

## 🎯 Why 8 Weeks?

**Reality Check:**
- Average job search (12+ years, India): 2-4 months
- Your target: 8 weeks = Aggressive but realistic
- Key: Quality over quantity (20 targeted apps beats 500 spray-and-pray)

**What You'll Do:**
- Week 1-2: Prepare (resume, LinkedIn, course)
- Week 3-4: Apply + Network (5 companies/week)
- Week 5-6: Interview (phone screens, technical rounds)
- Week 7-8: Final rounds + Negotiate offers

---

## 📊 The Numbers Game

### **Conversion Funnel (Realistic):**

```
100 applications
  ↓ 10% response rate
10 phone screens
  ↓ 50% pass rate
5 technical rounds
  ↓ 60% pass rate
3 final rounds
  ↓ 66% pass rate
2 offers

Result: 2% conversion rate (100 apps → 2 offers)
```

**Your Strategy:**
```
Target: 2-3 offers to negotiate between

Work Backwards:
  2 offers ← 3 final rounds ← 5 technical rounds ← 10 phone screens ← 100 apps?

NO! Use Referrals:
  2 offers ← 3 final rounds ← 5 technical rounds ← 10 phone screens ← 20 apps (with referrals)

Referral Boost:
  - Cold apply: 10% response rate
  - Referral: 50% response rate
  - 1 referral = 5 cold apps
```

**Key Insight:** 20 referrals beats 100 cold apps.

## 🚨 What If You're Not Getting Responses?

### **Problem 1: Resume Not Passing ATS**

**Fix:**
- Run through Jobscan.co (target 80%+ match)
- Add keywords from job posting
- Remove fancy formatting (plain text only)

---

### **Problem 2: No Referrals**

**Fix:**
- Expand LinkedIn network (connect with 50 engineers at target companies)
- Join Slack/Discord communities (ReactiFlux, Kafka users, etc.)
- Attend meetups (Bangalore: HasGeek, GDG, AWS meetups)

---

### **Problem 3: Failing Phone Screens**

**Fix:**
- Record yourself answering technical questions
- Practice more LeetCode Easy (solve 20 problems)
- Ask for feedback: "What could I improve?"

---

### **Problem 4: Failing Technical Rounds**

**Fix:**
- Hire a mock interview coach (Preplaced.in)
- Practice more system design (draw + explain 10 systems)
- Review your failures: What did they ask? What did you miss?
